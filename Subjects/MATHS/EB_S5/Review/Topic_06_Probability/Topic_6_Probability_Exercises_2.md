### Context for Exercises 1–3

A cinema has two screens: **Screen 1 (S1)** and **Screen 2 (S2)**.

There are **200 viewers** in total, of which **120 are watching Screen 1**.

Viewers are classified by age group:
- **C** = Children (under 12)
- **A** = Adults (12 to 64)
- **SE** = Seniors (65 and over)

Additional information:
- 40 children are watching Screen 1
- 50% of Screen 2 viewers are adults
- There are 30 seniors in total, one third of whom are watching Screen 2

One viewer is selected **at random**. The following events are considered:

- S1: "the viewer is watching Screen 1"
- C: "the viewer is a child"
- A: "the viewer is an adult"
- SE: "the viewer is a senior"

### Exercise 4 (6 points)

A factory produces components using two machines:
- **Machine A** produces **35%** of all components
- **Machine B** produces the remaining **65%**

Quality control shows that:
- **4%** of components from Machine A are **defective**
- **2%** of components from Machine B are **defective**

One component is selected at random from the production line.

Let:
- A = "the component was produced by Machine A"
- D = "the component is defective"

**a)** Represent this information on a **probability tree**, showing all branches and probabilities.

**b)** **Calculate** P(A ∩ D) — the probability that the component was made by Machine A **and** is defective.

**c)** **Calculate** P(D) — the probability that the component is defective. Use the **total probability formula**.

**d)** **Calculate** P(A | D) — the probability that the component came from Machine A, **given** that it is defective. **Interpret** this result.

**e)** **Determine** whether events A and D are **independent**. Justify your answer.
