### Context for Exercises 1–3

A sports club organises two types of activities: **Swimming (N)** and **Tennis (T)**.

The club has **150 members** in total, of which **90 are registered for swimming**.

Members are also grouped by age:
- **Y** = under 18 ("young")
- **A** = aged 18 to 40 ("adult")
- **S** = over 40 ("senior")

Additional information:
- 30 of the swimming members are in group Y
- 50% of the tennis members are in group A
- A total of 45 members are in group S, one-third of whom swim (N)

One member is selected **at random**.

---

### Exercise 1 (1 point)

**Complete** the following table:

|  | Y | A | S | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Swimming (N) | 30 | | | 90 |
| Tennis (T) | | | | |
| Total | | | 45 | 150 |

### Exercise 2 (1 point)

Give the answers as **simplified fractions**.

**Determine:**
- a) P(N) — the probability that the selected person does swimming
- b) P(S) — the probability that the selected person is in the senior group

---

### Exercise 3 (3 points)

Give the answers as **simplified fractions**.

- a) **Describe** the event N ∩ Y in one sentence. Then **calculate** P(N ∩ Y).
- b) **Calculate** P(N | Y) — the probability that a member does swimming, given that they are in the Y group. Interpret this result.
- c) **Calculate** P(S | T) — the probability that a member is senior, given they play tennis. Interpret this result in the context of the exercise.

### Exercise 4 (5 points)

In a leisure centre, **60% of members are women**. A survey shows that:
- 30% of women exercise more than 3 times per week
- 20% of men exercise more than 3 times per week

One member is selected at random.

Let:
- W = "the member is a woman"
- E = "the member exercises more than 3 times per week"

**a)** Represent this information on a **probability tree**.

**b)** **Calculate** P(W ∩ E) — the probability that the selected person is a woman who exercises more than 3 times per week.

**c)** **Calculate** P(E) — the probability that the selected person exercises more than 3 times per week.

**d)** **Determine** whether events W and E are **independent**. Justify your answer clearly.
